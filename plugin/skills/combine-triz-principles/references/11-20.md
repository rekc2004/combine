# Principles 11–20

| № | Principle | AI-task transformation |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Beforehand cushioning | Prepare emergency means for low reliability: fallback, backup, rollback, or a bounded safe branch before risk. |
| 12 | Equipotentiality | Change conditions so the object need not be raised/lowered: remove costly level changes, repeated serialization, and transfers; operate at the data's existing level. |
| 13 | The other way round | Reverse the prescribed action, swap moving/stationary roles, or invert the artifact: seek disproof, move the view instead of the data, reverse the operation. |
| 14 | Spheroidality | Use curved/rolling/rotating organization instead of linear organization: a circular flow whose output re-enters its input, or rotating perspectives. A one-way review is P23, not P14. |
| 15 | Dynamics | Make characteristics or movable parts adapt to each work phase: adaptive plan, prompt, limits, or interfaces. |
| 16 | Partial or excessive action | When 100% is hard, deliberately take slightly less or slightly more: a bounded 90% result with a remainder, or over-fetch followed by filtering. |
| 17 | Another dimension | Add a dimension or use the reverse side: line→matrix→graph, layered layout, multi-level representation, or a previously unused surface of the data. |
| 18 | Mechanical vibration | Vary the frequency of an action, induce oscillation, or tune to the system's natural/resonant rate: adaptive probe/retry cadence or parameter sweeps that amplify a measured response. Repetition alone is not resonance. |
| 19 | Periodic action | Replace continuous action with pulses, change their period, and use pauses for another useful action: scheduled checks or batched bursts with useful interleaving. |
| 20 | Continuity of useful action | Keep useful work continuous and remove idle/intermediate strokes: pipeline independent preparation while waiting, without fabricating dependencies. |

Gate: prefer one operator that creates a new degree of freedom; do not confuse vibration (P18), periodic action (P19), and feedback (P23).
