# A3–A4: IFR and Physical Contradiction

Prerequisite: OZ and OT from A2. If they are not recorded, read `01-frame.md` and complete A2 first; complete A1 too when the conflicting pair is unclear.

## A3. Ideal Final Result
**IFR-1:** "Within OZ during T1/T2, an X-change removes H and preserves U without complicating the system or creating new harm."

Strengthen it: use existing resources first; introduce no new component unless the task genuinely requires one. Necessary tests and verification do not violate ideality.

If the IFR remains abstract, use **size–time–cost**: ask what happens at zero and infinity for each, and identify the real dependency. Do not treat infinity as a practical requirement.

## A4. Physical contradiction and IFR-2
State opposite requirements to the **same element or zone**:
- "During OT, OZ must have state S to perform U, and must have the opposite state ¬S to avoid H."
- S may be large/small, present/absent, open/closed, centralized/distributed, or another qualitative state; it need not be numeric.
- When useful, add the microlevel: which smaller elements, events, tokens, or states must be present/absent to produce S and ¬S?

Then formulate **IFR-2**: "OZ itself provides S and ¬S at the required times/places/conditions, using available resources."

Gate: if the two demands do not apply to the same element, return to A1. If they do, propose a concrete separation or transformation and go to A9. Read `03-solve.md` only when no move emerges.
