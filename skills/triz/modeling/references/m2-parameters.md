# M2. AI-task Parameters

| # | Parameter | Worse | Better |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Result quality | Material errors | Required accuracy/depth |
| 2 | Task coverage | Missing requirements | Required scope covered |
| 3 | Verifiability | Judgment only | Explicit criterion/test passed |
| 4 | Cost | More tokens/calls/resources | Minimum for required quality |
| 5 | Response time | Excess waiting | Meets task deadline |
| 6 | Context occupancy | Needed evidence excluded | Needed evidence fits |
| 7 | Autonomy | Unnecessary confirmation loops | Carries to agreed criterion |
| 8 | Controllability | Hidden state/surprises | Visible plan, state, controls |
| 9 | Fault tolerance | One failure collapses work | Bounded/graceful degradation |
| 10 | Portability | Works only here | Reusable where needed |

Write: "Improving A (#) worsens B (#) because ___." The because-clause must identify the mechanism, not restate the trade-off.

Gate: use M3 for candidate transformations or A4 for opposite demands on one element. Do not force every nuisance into a physical contradiction.
